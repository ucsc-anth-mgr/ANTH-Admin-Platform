The undergraduate advisor reviews your submission first.

Student notifications

The message emailed to a student when their petition is returned for revision or completed. You are editing the introduction of each message — the essentials are always appended below it and cannot be edited away: the return note and resubmission link (returned); the per-course outcomes, the PDF link, and the Academic Advisement Report line (completed). Tokens {FirstName}, {Name}, and {Division} are filled in automatically.

Document access

Uploaded transcripts and syllabi, and every generated petition PDF, are shared file by file with the student who filed it and with everyone holding the undergraduate advisor or Director of Undergraduate Studies role. Sharing happens automatically at upload and at completion, using whoever holds those roles at that moment — so after adding someone to either role, run this once to give them access to documents from earlier petitions.
